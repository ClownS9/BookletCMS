# Page builder

Pages made of stacked sections, built on the page itself.

1. Switch it on in Settings › Inserts.
2. Open **Built pages** (in the Pages chapter), name a page and choose what to start from.
3. It opens on the site, ready to build:
   - **Click any words** to type into them.
   - **Click a section** to choose it: its tools appear beside it (move, copy, add one after it,
     its settings, take it out).
   - **The drawer** has everything else: buttons and where they go, pictures from Media, lists
     (cards, plans, questions), how the section looks (background, words left or centred,
     space, width), all the sections in order (drag to reorder), and the page's name, address
     and description.
   - **The bar** along the bottom: Undo, Phone view, Publish, Done.
4. Readers see nothing until you press **Publish**. After that, changes wait in the draft until
   you publish again (or go back to what's on the site).

Signed in, any built page has an **Edit this page** button in the corner. Readers never get the
button or the editor.

## From one of your pages

Under **Start from**, choose **One of your pages** and pick a page (or post). Its title, words,
headings, pictures and cut-outs become sections: a quote → Quotes, pictures → Pictures, two
columns → Columns, a call to action → Call to action, a video → Video, a tip → words in a box.
Nothing changes on the site until you publish. Then the built page takes the old page's address
and its place in your menus, and the old page is kept as a draft. Built from your home page, it
becomes the home page instead (Booklet 1.2), and the old one is left as it is.

Changed your mind? Page tab › **Put the old page back**.

## Sections

Hero · Words and a picture · Call to action · Cards · Columns · Questions · Pictures · Quotes ·
Video (YouTube or Vimeo, without tracking cookies) · Prices · Latest posts · Contact (the real
contact form; messages arrive in the Inbox).

Each one wears the site's theme (its colours and fonts), and fits phones.

## Elsewhere

- Any built page fits into a post or page: `[[builder: its-address]]`.
- Changing a published page's address keeps the old one working (it forwards).
- Stacy can tell you which pages there are and what's on them.
